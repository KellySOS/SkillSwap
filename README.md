# SkillSwap ⚡📱

> **Peer-to-Peer Campus Skill Exchange & Mentorship Platform**  
> Built for Apple iPhone 16 iOS 18 Design Specifications.

---

## 🌟 Overview

**SkillSwap** is a university-centric peer mentorship application where students trade skills directly through a mutual barter framework (`Wants ⇄ Gives`). No monetary transactions—just pure peer knowledge exchange!

- **Learn & Teach**: Trade Frontend Development for Machine Learning, UI/UX for Italian, and more.
- **Real-Time Mac Webcam Video Calls**: Native 1-on-1 WebRTC video mentorship with Picture-in-Picture selfie camera, live timer, and mic/cam controls.
- **Interactive Skills Library**: Add classes directly to your Library, Home carousel, and Campus feed.
- **Smart Explore & Hot Keywords**: Quick-filter by `UI/UX`, `Graphic Design`, `Python`, `Machine Learning`, `3D Blender`, and `Figma`.
- **Cross-Page Bookmarking**: Save any course from Explore directly to your "Me" page Bookmarks.
- **Gamified Streaks**: 7-day Monday–Sunday tracker, XP boosts, and level rewards.
